# Horizons — Rulebook v3 (transcribed)

Source: `Rulebook Horizons v3.zip` (6 rendered pages), downloaded 2026-11-24.
This file is the text source of truth for the rules; the PNGs are the layout.

## 1. Starting Horizons

Two-player strategy game with a shared deck of **45 action cards and 45 point cards**
(90 total). When the deck runs out, the horizon goes into the dusk, and the player with
the most points in their zenith wins.

Shuffle the deck and place it face down between the players. Each player draws a 5-card
hand. Randomly decide who goes first. **If you didn't go first, draw up to 5 cards at the
start of your first turn**, since you may use cards during your opponent's first turn.

Board layout: deck and dusk at the left, the horizon in the middle, each player's zenith
beside their own hand.

## 2. Turn structure

1. **Energize** cards by putting them from your hand into the dusk to gain **+3 energy per
   card**. Excess energy carries to later cards *this turn*. You can energize any time you
   can play a card.
2. **Play** a card from your hand to the horizon and pay its cost, shown at the top.
3. If your opponent passes, it **rises**: put it into the dusk if it's an action, into your
   zenith if it's a point, **then do its text**.
4. You can now play another card or end your turn. When you end your turn, **everyone loses
   all leftover energy**, and you **draw until you have five cards**.

## 3. Card types

- **Point** cards are how you win. As your points rise they go into your zenith, and the
  player with the most points in their zenith wins when the deck runs out.
  **You can only play points during your turn while the horizon is empty.**
- **Action** cards let you interact with your opponent. When your action rises it goes into
  the dusk, then you do its text.
  **You can play actions on your turn OR in response to an opponent's card.**

## 4. The horizon

- **Playing:** cards don't rise as soon as you play them. Each card goes face-up onto the
  horizon, **overlapping the previous card on its right. The rightmost card is the top.**
- **Responding:** whenever a player plays a card to the horizon, **their opponent** may
  respond with an action or pass. If they play a card, the initial player can then respond.
- **Rising:** **if the opponent passes, that card rises.** Move it to the dusk if it's an
  action, or to its controller's zenith if it's a point. Then do its text. The next card
  down becomes the new top, and **the opponent of the player who played it** may respond to
  it or pass. Keep going until the horizon is empty.
- **Removing:** some cards remove other cards from the horizon. The card says where it goes,
  and **it never rises**.

## 5. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Horizon | The place between players where cards go before they rise. |
| Rise | When a card rises, move it to the dusk or your zenith, then do its text. |
| Respond | Playing a card on top of an opponent's card before it rises. |
| Pass | Declining to respond to a card on the horizon. Passing lets a card rise. |
| Action | Playable on your turn or in response to an opponent's card. |
| Point | Locked to your turn while the horizon is empty. More points let you win. |
| Controller | The player who played a card controls it and its effects. |
| Dusk | The shared, face-up pile. Energized cards and risen actions go here. |
| Energy | What a card costs. Energize a card in your hand to gain 3 energy for this turn. |
| Zenith | Your face-up pile of points that have risen. |
| Play | Putting a card on the horizon. A card is played even if it doesn't rise. |

## 6. Sunset

When the deck runs out, the game ends. If a card is rising, finish it first, then put the
rest of the horizon into the dusk. The player with the most points in their zenith wins.
