## Reconciliation against the current 105-card set

90 cards: 45 points (`00`–`44`) and 45 actions (`45`–`89`). 86 carry over from the current
set, **4 are new**, **19 are dropped**, and **no carried-over card changed its energy cost**.

Six of the 90 filenames name a different card than the art shows, so the filename is not a
usable key: `22Treasure_Anew` is Offer Perspective, `26Birth_the_Sky` is Treasure Anew,
`32Stumble` is Avoid Conflict, `47Reverse` is Past Passion, `87Settle` is Cerebral Snuff and
`89Gamble` is Unwind Intent. Four of those six names (Stumble, Reverse, Settle, Gamble) are
cards that were dropped, and their names were left on slots now holding something else.

### Dropped (19)

Strafe, Forever Borrow and Birth the Sky going confirms the points-in-response subsystem is
dead; nothing left in the set grants it.

### Text changes that are mechanical, not wording (16)

42 of the 86 carried-over cards changed text, but most are 2-player cleanup
("opponents" → "your opponent"), the void → **energize** rename, or "costs 2 energy less" →
"costs 2 less energy". These are the ones that change what the card does:

| id | name | change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 13 | Efficiency | discount narrowed from all your opponent's cards to their **actions** |
| 14 | Pilfer Thoughts | "reveal a player's hand" → your opponent reveals and **you** choose |
| 15 | Overwhelm | discount now counts **cards played this turn**, not cards on the horizon; response-only clause dropped |
| 19 | Endure Hatred | trigger moves from "opponent plays a card" to "your opponent's **action rises**" |
| 21 | Paranoia | locks you out of **actions** rather than all cards |
| 26 | Treasure Anew | redesigned: was a static horizon trigger, now a rise effect (hand → dusk, redraw that many) |
| 27 | Sow | triggers on an **action** being played, not any card |
| 33 | Consult The Past | surcharge drops from +2 to **+1** energy |
| 53 | Blinding Flash | redesigned: was "play points in response to actions", now "your opponent can't respond to your cards this turn" |
| 57 | Light's Guidance | counts points in **your** zenith only, not both zeniths |
| 65 | Inspect | the rest go to the **dusk** instead of the bottom of the deck |
| 66 | Enlightenment | redesigned into a ransom: the horizon card dusks **unless its controller pays** the discarded card's cost |
| 68 | Avert Injustice | blocks **any** response to your next action, not just action responses |
| 81 | Possess Love | was "both draw two", now you draw two and your opponent draws one |
| 87 | Cerebral Snuff | was a random discard, now reveal your opponent's hand and choose |
| 44 | Dawn | the 0-energy discount becomes optional ("you may play this for 0") |

Two more look like changes but are not: **00 Pretty Privilege** ("can't play cards" → "can't
play actions") and **30 Beget Advantage** ("four or more points" → "three or more *other*
points") both describe the same game state once the points-in-response cards are gone.
**41 Pride** ("if it rises" → "if it's in your zenith") is the same clarification.
